What this tool is

This tool lets you compare a legal invoice against General Legal's published flat fees. It is an illustrative cost comparison and general information only. It is not legal advice, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship.

How your document is handled

There are two modes, and you choose which one:

  • On-device (default): your PDF is read and analyzed entirely inside your web browser. The file and its contents are never transmitted to us or to any third party, and nothing is stored.
  • Analyze with AI (opt-in): when you check this box, the extracted invoice text (not the file), with the letterhead and obvious identifiers removed on your device first, is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to generate your result. We do not write it to a database or retain it after the response is returned.

Redaction

Before any text is sent in AI mode, the tool attempts to remove the document header and obvious identifiers (email addresses, phone numbers, links, and long identification numbers) in your browser. This is a best-effort measure and not a guarantee. Please do not upload privileged or confidential information, and only upload documents you have the right to share.

Your email address

To view a comparison, you enter your email address. By continuing, you agree that General Legal may use that email to send your results and to contact you about its services. Your email is submitted to our server and stored so we can follow up. This applies even when the invoice itself is analyzed on your device.
