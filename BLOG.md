# Introducing Call BS

We built a small tool that reads a legal invoice and shows you, in plain numbers, what a comparable scope of work would cost at General Legal's flat rates. It's live today at billcheck.general.legal, and the code is public on GitHub.

Upload a bill, or run the sample invoice built into the tool if you don't have one handy, and it walks the invoice entry by entry, matches each line to our published flat-fee schedule, and puts both totals side by side so you can see exactly which line items make up the difference. By default nothing about your bill leaves your device. If you turn on the optional AI analysis, it's the extracted text only, with obvious identifiers stripped out first, never the original file.

A quick note about the name: we called it Call BS because the exercise of lining up what a document is worth against what it costs by the hour is, we think, worth taking that seriously.

## Why hourly bills run long

Paying for time instead of results means every manual step, drafting from scratch, tracking down precedent, redlining a clause by hand, becomes its own billable line. That's not usually anyone acting in bad faith. It's what time-based pricing does whenever the underlying work is still done by hand, one document at a time.

A lot of that repetitive, mechanical effort is exactly what AI now compresses into minutes. Flat pricing goes a step further: once a fee is fixed up front, there's no arithmetic reward for anything taking longer.

## Where AI still needs a lawyer

None of this means AI is ready to practice on its own, because it isn't. There's a lot of legal work where a sharp attorney will run circles around a model left unsupervised, and this tool isn't trying to replace that judgment. It's here to show you what a comparable flat fee looks like side by side with an existing bill, not to give legal advice.

That's the premise behind General Legal: pairing an experienced attorney with modern tooling produces better work, faster, and lets us hand the savings back to clients as a fixed price instead of a running clock.
